Android NetBoot modifies the volume entry point to discover a physical partition
using the library's existing -1 selector rather than hardcoding partition zero.
The exact patch and source archive SHA-256 are recorded in
app/src/main/cpp/CMakeLists.txt. Partition-map validation is retained.
